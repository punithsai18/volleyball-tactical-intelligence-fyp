01 • GOALS

Project Objectives

Core engineering and research benchmarks targeted by the VolleyTactix AI framework

🎯

Autonomous Tactical Recognition

Eliminate manual video tagging by automatically classifying high-level collective team tactics across 948 labelled clips from 11 broadcast matches using 29-frame sliding windows without sensors.

🤖

Markerless Multi-Object Vision

Deploy YOLO11m-pose with BoT-SORT tracking to extract 17 keypoints per player alongside a fine-tuned detector (volleyball_best.pt) finding the ball at 1280 px with median court homography.

🧠

16-Model Benchmarking

Provide an honest empirical benchmark comparing 12 Machine Learning models and 4 Deep Learning sequence models (InceptionTime, TCN, BiLSTM, Mamba SSM) under leave-one-video-out cross-validation.

⚡

Real-Time Invariant Geometry

Formulate identity-stable slots and scale-invariant descriptors (Minimum Spanning Tree, Convex Hull, centroid velocity, dispersion variance) achieving real-time inference latency under 18ms.

02 • MOTIVATION

Problem Statement

Why traditional sports analysis workflows fail modern competitive requirements

Core Challenge: In modern high-performance volleyball, ball velocities frequently exceed 120 km/h, and player transitions occur in under 400 milliseconds. Current tactical intelligence relies almost entirely on manual, post-game scouting software (e.g., DataVolley, VolleyStation), requiring trained statisticians to manually record key events over 4 to 6 hours per match. This creates a severe operational bottleneck, introduces human perceptual bias, and remains financially inaccessible to grassroots, collegiate, and academy programs.

03 • TECHNICAL PIPELINE

Methodology & Mathematical Formulation

End-to-end multi-stage pipeline from broadcast pixels to consensus tactical decisions

01

Broadcast Stream Ingestion

Extract raw match video streams at 30 FPS. Construct rolling temporal sequences of 29 frames (~0.96 seconds), perfectly capturing the kinetic build-up from setter reception to net spike/block.

V = \{ I_t \}_{t=1}^{29}, \quad I_t \in \mathbb{R}^{H \times W \times 3}
02

Dual YOLO Vision Tracking

Simultaneously deploy YOLOv11-Pose for 17 skeletal joint keypoints across all 6 court players alongside a fine-tuned specialized volleyball detection model with Kalman trajectory smoothing.

\mathcal{K}_t = \{ (x_i, y_i, c_i) \}_{i=1}^{17 \times 6}, \quad \mathcal{B}_t = (x_b, y_b)
03

Planar Homography Normalization

Calibrate 4 ground-truth FIVB court corners. Apply perspective inversion to transform distorted broadcast pixels $(u, v)$ into metric coordinates $(X, Y) \in [0, 9] \times [0, 18]$ meters.

\begin{bmatrix} X \\ Y \\ 1 \end{bmatrix} \sim \mathbf{H} \begin{bmatrix} u \\ v \\ 1 \end{bmatrix}, \quad \mathbf{H} \in \mathbb{R}^{3 \times 3}
04

18-Dim Geometric Feature Engine

Derive frame-level invariant spatial features: Minimum Spanning Tree (MST) edge length, Convex Hull territorial envelope area and perimeter, team centroid kinematics, dispersion variance, and ball proximity.

\mathbf{f}_t = [\text{MST}, \mathcal{A}_{\text{hull}}, \mathcal{P}_{\text{hull}}, \bar{x}, \bar{y}, v_x, v_y, \sigma_d, \dots] \in \mathbb{R}^{18}
05

13-Model Zoo Classification

Evaluate sequences across 9 Machine Learning (HistGradientBoosting, ExtraTrees, GradientBoosting, RBF-SVM, RandomForest, MLP, k-NN, Linear SVM, LogisticRegression) and 4 Deep Learning sequence models (InceptionTime, TCN, BiLSTM+Attention, Mamba SSM).

P(y = c \mid \mathbf{F}_{1:29}) = \text{Softmax}(\mathbf{W} \cdot \Phi(\mathbf{F}_{1:29}))
06

Ensemble Consensus & Telemetry

Perform weighted decision arbiter across ML and DL models. Output confidence margin, model agreement percentage, and stream real-time interactive 2D court telemetry with velocity vectors.

\hat{y} = \arg\max_{c} \sum_{m=1}^{13} w_m \cdot P_m(y = c)
